# Portrait de la Grosse Dame

- Outil : imagegen intégré, génération originale, sans image de référence.
- Fichier utilisé par le site : `14/assets/grosse-dame.webp`.
- Dimensions : 1086 × 1448 pixels.
- Le fichier PNG original généré est conservé dans le dossier de génération de Codex ; l’asset WebP du projet est une conversion de format, sans retouche de l’image.
- Le portrait est généré ; il ne s’agit pas d’un visuel officiel ou d’un extrait de film. Le cadre et le décor sont créés en CSS et SVG.

## Prompt final

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait painting inside an interactive Harry Potter inspired password-door web game. Primary request: the Fat Lady, the kindly imperious, plump middle-aged lady guarding a magical school common room, painted as a richly textured 18th century oil portrait. Subject: full-figured woman seated three-quarter length, small dark brown ringlets in an updo, rose and wine-colored silk gown with puffed sleeves and lace neckline, pearl necklace, holding a delicate crystal goblet in one hand, other hand resting on her lap, knowing raised eyebrow and playful closed-mouth smile, looking directly at the viewer. Scene: dark olive and warm umber painted background, faded burgundy drapery at left, soft candle illumination. Composition: vertical 3:4 portrait, entire head, hands, goblet and upper skirt fully visible, centered with breathing room. Fine realistic brushwork, old varnish, woven canvas texture, chiaroscuro, warm skin tones, museum quality portrait with whimsical character. No external frame, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no UI. This is the portrait canvas only; frame will be implemented separately in the website.
